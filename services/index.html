Consulting FAQs

What is fractional consulting?

Fractional consultants provide expertise and strategic guidance on a part-time or project basis. Clients can thus access expertise without committing to full-time hires while getting support in the long term. For example, I typically work with my clients between one hour and half-a-day a week, over several months, to up-skill their team and develop their projects.

How do I choose the right consultant?

You need to assess consultants based on their experience, expertise and credentials. Verify that the consultant’s specific expertise aligns with your project needs, ensure they have relevant industry knowledge and experience, and look for proven track record in solving similar problems. To review my profile, feel free to Google my name, visit the credentials and testimonials pages on this website, and do not hesitate to contact me.

How to start a consultancy programme with you?

I am flexible and it usually starts with a call or a visit to your office. I first ask a lot of questions to try to understand your current needs and make suggestions on how to start depending on what I learn and based on my experience working with other clients. For clients with no-prior expertise in Data Science or AI, I suggest to start with an AI audit, where I spend a couple of hours talking to various stakeholders in the business and suggest possible projects we could work on, each attached with a level of difficulty and a timescale. We usually continue with a 12 week programme, one hour per week, where we initiate a first project with a small team to get to know each others on a concrete project. Based on those results we decide on the next steps. If you have a clear idea of your needs from the onset, I often work asynchronously on research projects, act as a part-time CTOs, or organise a larger team of experts from my network to dive deep into your cutting-edge project.

How are consultant fees structured?

I work on a weekly retainer or an hourly rate. In some cases, I can work for equity. I regularly sign NDAs with clients to ensure all work is protected and I have suitable professional insurance.
