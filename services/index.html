Consulting FAQs

What is fractional consulting?

Senior expertise on a part-time basis, over months rather than in a single burst. You get someone senior in the room without committing to a full-time hire. I typically work with clients between one hour and one full day a week, over several months, to upskill their team and develop their projects.

How do I choose the right consultant?

Check that the expertise actually matches your problem, that there is relevant experience behind it, and that there is a track record on similar problems. Google my name, and read the credentials and testimonials pages on this site.

How does an engagement usually start?

With a call, or a visit to your office, where I ask a lot of questions. For teams with no prior AI experience I usually suggest an audit first, then a first project with a small team at one or two hours a week, and we decide the next step from the result. If you already know what you need, I also work asynchronously on research problems, act as a part-time CTO, or pull in other experts from my network.

How are fees structured?

Invoiced monthly for the time actually delivered and agreed in advance. There is nothing to pay for a week I am not working. In some cases I can work for equity. I sign NDAs as a matter of course and carry professional indemnity insurance.

Does this qualify for R&D tax relief?

Often, yes. For UK companies, qualifying external consultancy spend on genuinely novel technical work can attract relief, which describes most of what I am hired for. Worth putting to your accountant before you budget.
