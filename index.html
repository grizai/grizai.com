How a few hours a week is enough

We meet weekly

Same slot, every week. We look at what happened, decide what matters next, and set a goal your team can hit before we meet again. Progress comes from the cadence, not from long workshops.

I work between sessions

Reading your data, testing an approach, reviewing a paper you’re relying on, writing code where it’s faster than explaining. Hard problems need time to think, so you get judgement that has had a week to form rather than an opinion produced on the spot.

Your team does the building

I’m not a bottleneck and I don’t hold the keys. Every decision gets explained, so the reasoning stays with your engineers after I leave.

Most engagements run six months or more. Not because the work never ends, but because new questions keep arriving and it’s cheaper to have someone who already knows your context.
